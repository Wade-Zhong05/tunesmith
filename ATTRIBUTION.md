**Ensemble (multi-instrument) mode (AI-written at the student's request, 2026-11-22).**
- Planned with the student, then implemented by Claude Code:
  - `src/tunesmith/music/ensemble.py`;
  - the ensemble parts of `attributes.py`, `tokenizer.py`, `model/generate.py`,
    `eval/metrics.py`, `render/` and `pipeline.py`;
  - parser support for instrument lists and drums;
  - the web UI's ensemble controls;
  - `configs/*ensemble*`, `tests/test_ensemble.py`.
- The representation follows miditok's REMI with Program tokens (one interleaved stream),
  informed by the Multitrack Music Transformer (Dong et al., ICASSP 2023) and by Text2midi.
- Problems found while validating:
  - miditok decodes at its own tick resolution, so round-trip tests compare beats, not
    ticks;
  - without a cap, unconstrained ensembles used up to 22 instruments; the grammar now
    limits unspecified requests to four families.

**Sound and sampling fixes (AI-written at the student's request, 2026-11-23),** after the
student reported that the melodies sounded odd:
- `src/tunesmith/model/sampling_filters_ref.py`: reference temperature, top-k and top-p
  filters, used only while the student's `sampling_filters.py` raises
  `NotImplementedError`. Before this, the temperature and top-p settings were ignored.
- A `tinysoundfont` rendering backend in `render/render.py`, because symusic's SoundFont
  synthesizer fails on Windows; the numpy synthesizer stays as the fallback.
- A rest limit in the generation grammar (`model/generate.py`): a piece starts with notes
  in bar 1 and has at most one silent bar in a row. Without it the melody model sometimes
  wrote 9 to 11 empty bars before the first note. `run_eval.py --max-empty-bars -1`
  turns the limit off for comparison.
- `src/tunesmith/eval/listening.py`: blind A/B listening pages comparing real excerpts
  with generated ones under different sampling settings.
- Spelling repair in the rule parser (`understand/rules.py`, `normalize_text`), after the
  student's request "a very very saaddd and very sloooow song with violin piano and
  guita" lost its mood and tempo:
  - stretched letters are squeezed back to a keyword;
  - a single missing, extra or swapped letter in a longer word is corrected.
  It changes no word in the parser test set, so the rule-parser results are unchanged.
- The app now reads descriptions with the LoRA parser instead of the keyword rules.
  - The same spelling repair runs before the language model.
  - Parse results are cached per description.

### What the AI tool produced, and what had to be fixed

Problems found and fixed while the framework was being built and validated, all by
running the code:
- miditok counts beats in units of the time-signature denominator, so a 6/8 bar has 48
  positions, not 24. The grammar's bar-length formula was corrected after a 6/8
  round-trip test.
- Generated melodies could ring past the last bar (16 bars requested, 19–20 measured),
  so notes are now clipped at the final bar line, and bars are counted from the
  generated Bar tokens.
- Requests without a meter could produce rare time signatures (for example 5/4). The
  grammar now restricts unspecified meters to 4/4, 3/4, 2/4 and 6/8.
- `build_model` passed `vocab_size` twice to the Transformer config. This was found by
  testing the training path against a throwaway reference implementation, which was then
  deleted so it is not part of the repository.
- A regex rewrite lost its `\b` word boundaries; this was fixed and covered by the
  rule-parser tests.
- Training data with 2% validation files had no validation split on small datasets, so a
  `--val-fraction` option was added.
- The first full preparation run crashed on files with unusual time signatures (e.g.
  5/4). The meter validator rejected the value "other" that the label mapping itself
  produces. It now accepts it. A malformed metadata row now skips that file instead of
  stopping the run, and a test covers both cases.

## Ideas and methods from prior work

- REMI representation: Huang & Yang, "Pop Music Transformer" (ACM MM 2020).
- Attribute-conditioned two-stage text-to-music: MuseCoco (Lu et al., 2023).
- End-to-end text-to-MIDI baseline and metrics (CLAP score, compression ratio): Text2midi
  (Bhandari et al., AAAI 2025).
- Classifier-free guidance: Ho & Salimans (2022).
- Nucleus sampling: Holtzman et al. (ICLR 2020).
- Key finding: Krumhansl & Kessler key profiles (1982) with the Krumhansl-Schmuckler
  algorithm.
- LoRA: Hu et al. (ICLR 2022).
- Diffusion models: Ho, Jain & Abbeel, "Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models"
  (NeurIPS 2020); Nichol & Dhariwal, "Improved DDPM" (ICML 2021, cosine schedule);
  Dhariwal & Nichol, "Diffusion Models Beat GANs" (NeurIPS 2021, U-Net with scale-shift
  conditioning); Song, Meng & Ermon, "DDIM" (ICLR 2021); U-Net: Ronneberger et al.
  (MICCAI 2015).
- Inpainting by replacing the known region at each step: Song et al., "Score-Based
  Generative Modeling through SDEs" (ICLR 2021); Lugmayr et al., "RePaint" (CVPR 2022).
- Piano-roll diffusion with onset and sustain channels: Min, Jiang, Xia & Zhao,
  "Polyffusion" (ISMIR 2023).
